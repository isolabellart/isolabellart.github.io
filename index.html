05THE NETWORK UNDERNEATH

Not owned by one.
Open to everyone.

Postilla is built on Nostr, an open protocol for social networking. Your identity is based on cryptographic keys, and your posts are shared through independent relays. You can take your identity to other compatible clients.

Nostr is a distributed network. Event availability and delivery depend on the relays used by you and by the people you follow.
